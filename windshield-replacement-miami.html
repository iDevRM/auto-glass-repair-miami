What Does Windshield Replacement Involve?

The job starts with covers over the hood, fenders and dash. The technician cuts the old glass free from its urethane bond and trims the old adhesive down to a thin, even layer. Any bare metal on the frame is primed so rust cannot start under the new seal, which matters with salt air coming off Biscayne Bay. Then fresh urethane goes down, the new windshield is set and centered, and the mirror, sensors and moldings go back on. Plan on about an hour, plus cure time. (If your car has a heads-up display, mention it when you call. That windshield is a different part.)

When Do You Need Windshield Replacement?

A crack longer than a dollar bill

Long cracks cannot be stopped with resin and keep running.

Damage in the driver’s view

Resin leaves a faint mark, so breaks in front of the driver need new glass.

A crack at the edge

Edge cracks weaken the bond between glass and frame and spread fast.

Several chips at once

Three or more chips, common for I-95 and Palmetto commuters, add up to weak glass.

A leak in a storm

Water on the dash after a downpour often points to a failed seal.

Hurricane or storm damage

A falling branch or flying debris can cave in the glass in one hit.

Why Do Windshield Cracks Spread Faster in Miami Heat?

A windshield is two layers of glass with a clear plastic layer in between, and a rock chip breaks the outer layer. Glass grows as it heats and shrinks as it cools, and every swing pulls at the ends of the break. In Miami the worst moment is getting into a car that sat in an open lot all afternoon and putting the A/C on full, cold air on the inside, hot glass on the outside. A summer storm dropping cool rain on a hot windshield does the same thing. A chip picked up in May is often a crack across the glass by August.

What Affects the Cost of Windshield Replacement in Miami?

The first thing most callers ask is “Will my insurance cover this?” With comprehensive coverage, Florida Statute 627.7288 keeps the deductible off windshield damage, so a covered claim usually costs you nothing. Without insurance, the cost of windshield replacement in Miami depends mostly on what is built into the glass. Frequently Asked Questions

How soon can I drive after a new windshield?

Most urethane adhesives reach safe drive-away strength in one to two hours in Miami weather. The technician gives you the exact time before leaving. Driving too soon can let the glass shift or weaken the bond the airbag relies on.

Is OEM glass better than aftermarket glass?

OEM glass comes from the same supplier that made your original windshield. Good aftermarket glass meets the same federal safety rules, but on cars with cameras or heads-up displays OEM glass can make calibration easier. We tell you which options fit your vehicle and your insurance.

When can I wash my car after a windshield replacement?

Wait at least a day before a car wash, and keep pressure washers away from the edges of the glass for a couple of days so the new seal is not disturbed. A normal rain shower is fine once the car is ready to drive.

Does Florida’s zero-deductible law cover my new windshield?

If you carry comprehensive coverage and the damage is to the windshield, Florida Statute 627.7288 says the deductible does not apply. It does not cover side windows or back glass. We can check your coverage when we quote.
