The basics

What Is Auto Glass Repair in Miami?

Auto glass repair in Miami covers every piece of glass on a car, truck or SUV. That means filling rock chips with resin, putting in a new windshield, replacing a side window or back glass, and re-aiming the safety camera that looks out through the front glass. Drivers on I-95, the Dolphin Expressway and the Palmetto share the road with dump trucks and cement mixers feeding the towers going up in Brickell and Downtown.

A windshield is a safety part, too. It is glued to the body with urethane, it helps hold the roof up in a rollover and the passenger airbag pushes against it, so how it goes in matters as much as the glass itself.

When Do You Need Windshield Repair or Replacement in Miami?

Most calls start with one of these. If one sounds like your car, get the glass looked at soon.

Cost and insurance

How Much Does Windshield Replacement Cost in Miami?

For most insured drivers, the windshield itself costs nothing out of pocket. Florida Statute 627.7288 says the deductible on comprehensive coverage does not apply to windshield damage, so if your policy carries comprehensive coverage, a covered windshield claim in Miami is paid without a deductible. Most people are surprised by that. The law covers the windshield only. Door glass, quarter glass and back glass still go through your normal deductible.

Without insurance, the cost of windshield replacement in Miami depends mostly on what is built into the glass. Rain sensors, acoustic layers, heads-up display glass and solar-tinted glass need more specialized parts, and a forward camera adds a calibration step. Call with your year, make and model and we will give you a free quote and check your coverage before any work starts.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Auto Glass in Miami

The question we hear most on the phone is “Is my windshield really free with insurance?” It is the first answer below.

Does insurance pay for windshield replacement in Florida?

Usually, yes. Florida Statute 627.7288 says the deductible on comprehensive coverage does not apply to the windshield, so if you carry comprehensive coverage, a covered windshield claim in Miami normally costs you nothing out of pocket. The waiver covers the windshield only. Side windows and back glass go through your regular deductible.

Can I get a ticket for a cracked windshield in Miami?

You can. Florida police can stop a car for unsafe equipment, and a crack across the driver’s view is the kind that draws attention. A small chip off to the side rarely causes trouble, but a long crack in front of the steering wheel is worth fixing before it turns into a ticket.

Can a windshield chip be repaired instead of replaced?

Often it can. A chip smaller than a quarter, or a single crack shorter than about six inches, can usually be filled with resin if it sits away from the edge and out of the driver’s direct view. Send us the size and where it is on the glass and we will tell you which fix fits.

Why do windshield chips spread so fast in Miami?

Heat is the main reason. A windshield parked in the Miami sun gets very hot, and when the A/C blasts cold air at the inside, the two sides of the glass pull against each other and the chip runs. Summer thunderstorms dumping cool rain on hot glass do the same thing.

Do you come to my condo or office garage?

Yes. Miami Auto Glass Repair is a mobile service, so the technician works in your driveway, a condo garage in Brickell or the lot at your job. Some buildings have rules for vendors or low ceilings, so tell us the building when you book. The address on this site is a service office, not a shop.

How long does a windshield replacement take?

The install takes about an hour. After that the urethane adhesive needs roughly one to two hours before the car is safe to drive, and the technician gives you the exact time before leaving. A chip repair is much quicker, usually about 30 minutes.

What happens if it rains during my appointment?

Wet glass and a wet frame make a weak bond, so we never set a windshield in the rain. Miami storms tend to roll in during summer afternoons, which is why we book mornings when we can and work inside covered garages whenever one is available.
