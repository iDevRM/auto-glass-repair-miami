What Does Windshield Chip Repair Involve?

First the chip gets cleaned out and any moisture from the humidity or the last storm is dried. A bridge tool is mounted over the damage with suction cups. It pulls the air out of the break, then pushes clear resin in under pressure. A UV lamp cures the resin hard, and the surface is scraped and polished flat. In bright Miami sun the work is done in shade, since direct sunlight can start curing the resin before it fills the break. Most chips end up much harder to see, and they stop spreading.

When Do You Need Windshield Chip Repair?

Short crack off a chip

A chip before summer

Leased car with a chip

A clean repair helps avoid a charge for damaged glass at turn-in.

Fresh hit from the highway

Gravel from trucks on I-95, I-395 and SR 836 is the usual cause.

A chip outside the driver’s view

Repairs leave a faint mark, so they work best away from the driver’s eye line.

Why Does a Small Windshield Chip Turn Into a Crack?

A chip is a break in the outer layer of glass, and every change in temperature stresses its edges. Parked in the open, a Miami windshield gets hot enough to burn your hand, and the A/C cools the inside fast once you start driving. Potholes, speed bumps in parking garages and slammed doors add shocks. People ask, “Can it wait a few months?” Sometimes. Through a Miami summer, usually not.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a windshield chip repair take?

Most chip repairs take about 30 minutes on site, and you can drive right away. The resin is cured with UV light, so there is no waiting for it to dry.

Will the chip disappear completely?

Usually it gets much harder to see, but a faint mark often stays. The main goal is to stop the chip from spreading and give the glass back its strength.

What should I do with a chip until it is fixed?

Put clear tape over the chip to keep dirt and rain out, park in the shade when you can, and let the A/C come up slowly instead of aiming it at the glass on full. That gives the chip the best chance of staying small until the repair.

Can a chip be repaired in the rain or bright sun?

Yes, as long as the chip is dry and the glass is shaded. The technician works under a garage, carport or cover so water does not get trapped under the resin and the sun does not cure it too early.
